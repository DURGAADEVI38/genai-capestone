## Learning Delivery Methods

### Synchronous (Live)

**Instructor-Led Courses**
- Weekly scheduled sessions
- Live interaction with instructors
- Q&A and discussion opportunities
- Certificates upon completion
- Average 8-20 hours per course

**Workshops & Bootcamps**
- Intensive 2-5 day programs
- Hands-on practical experience
- Small cohort sizes (8-15 people)
- Conducted quarterly
- 40-80 hours typical

**Peer Learning Sessions**
- 1-hour monthly sessions
- Employees share expertise
- Rotating facilitators
- Informal and collaborative
- No certification

### Asynchronous (Self-Paced)

**Video-Based Courses**
- Access anytime, anywhere
- Average 5-40 hours per course
- Video + quizzes + assessments
- Providers: Coursera, Udemy, Pluralsight
- Company-subsidized costs

**Reading & Documentation**
- Curated reading lists
- Internal documentation
- Research papers
- Industry blogs and publications
- Average 10-30 hours per topic

**Hands-On Labs**
- Virtual lab environments
- Real-world scenario practice
- Available 24/7
- Platforms: Linux Academy, A Cloud Guru
- For infrastructure and cloud training

### Hybrid

**Blended Learning Programs**
- Combination of online + in-person
- Practical projects between sessions
- Coaching and mentorship
- Most effective for complex skills
- 6-16 weeks typical duration

**Communities of Practice**
- Ongoing groups around topics
- Monthly virtual meetings
- Shared resources and knowledge base
- Mentorship relationships
- Active: DevOps, Machine Learning, Design Systems

## Career Development Paths

### Engineering Paths

**Individual Contributor Track**
- Software Engineer → Senior Engineer → Staff Engineer → Principal Engineer
- Advancement based on technical expertise and impact
- Expected years at each level: 2-3 years, 3-4 years, 4+ years

**Management Track**
- Software Engineer → Engineering Manager → Senior Manager → Director
- Requires people management skills
- Training in leadership, hiring, and team development
- Salary parity maintained with IC track

**Specialist Track**
- Deep expertise in specific areas
- Security Engineer, Data Engineer, Infrastructure Architect
- Recognized as domain experts
- Mentoring and consultation roles

### Product & Design Paths

**Product Track**
- Associate PM → Product Manager → Senior PM → Principal PM
- Advancement based on business impact
- Skills: Strategy, analytics, communication

**Design Track**
- Designer → Senior Designer → Design Lead → Design Director
- Advancement based on design impact and leadership
- Skills: Craft, systems thinking, team building

### Management Ladders

**People Management**
- Manager of Managers
- Director
- VP
- C-Suite
- Typical progression: 3-4 years per level

### Lateral Moves

- Encouraged at any career stage
- Broadens perspective and skills
- Can accelerate career growth
- 1-year stability expected before next move

## Training Budget & Reimbursement

### Annual Training Budget

- All employees: $2,000/year base budget
- Additional budget based on level and role
- Unused budget does NOT roll over
- Manager pre-approval required

### Eligible Expenses

- **Fully Covered**:
  - Online courses relevant to role
  - Company-sponsored bootcamps
  - Industry conferences
  - Professional certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure, etc.)

- **Partially Covered (50-75%)**:
  - Online courses for adjacent skills
  - Books and learning materials
  - Professional memberships (ACM, IEEE, APA, etc.)

- **NOT Covered**:
  - Degrees (see tuition reimbursement)
  - Travel costs (separate from budget)
  - Personal development unrelated to role

### Tuition Reimbursement

- Up to $5,000/year for job-related degrees
- Bachelor's, Master's, or professional certifications
- Minimum 2.5 GPA/grade requirement
- Must be relevant to current or planned role
- Reimbursed after course completion with passing grade
- 2-year tenure commitment required

### Conference & Event Attendance

- Annual allocation based on role and level
- Company covers registration + hotel + meals
- Flights covered up to $1,000 (higher with VP approval)
- Personal days at conference not counted as PTO
- Post-conference knowledge sharing required
