# TechNova Solutions HR Policies & Procedures

## Welcome to TechNova Solutions

TechNova Solutions is a leading technology company committed to innovation, excellence, and employee well-being.

## Employment Policies

### Employment Classification

Employees are classified as either:
- **Full-Time**: 40+ hours per week with full benefits
- **Part-Time**: Less than 40 hours per week with prorated benefits
- **Contractor**: Temporary engagement on a project basis

### Equal Opportunity Employment

TechNova Solutions is an Equal Opportunity Employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

## Compensation & Benefits

### Salary Reviews

- Annual salary reviews are conducted in December
- Performance bonuses are awarded based on individual and company performance
- Salary adjustments are based on market data, performance, and tenure

### Health Insurance

- Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance
- Coverage begins on the first day of employment
- Company covers 80% of employee premiums, 60% of dependent premiums
- Annual open enrollment in November

### Retirement Benefits

- 401(k) plan with company match up to 4% of salary
- Immediate eligibility for new hires
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, 100% after 4 years

### Paid Time Off (PTO)

- **Vacation Days**:
  - Employees get 15 days of paid vacation annually
  - Additional day granted for each 5 years of service (max 25 days)
  - Unused vacation can be carried over (max 5 days) to next year

- **Sick Days**: 10 days annually for personal illness or family care
  
- **Holidays**: 10 company holidays observed plus 2 floating holidays
  - New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day
  - Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (2 days), Christmas

- **Parental Leave**: 
  - 12 weeks paid leave for birth/adoption
  - Additional 4 weeks unpaid leave available

### Professional Development

- Annual learning budget: $2,000 per employee
- Tuition reimbursement up to $5,000/year for job-related education
- Conference and training program sponsorship
- Internal mentorship programs
- Technical certification support

## Leave Policies

### Medical Leave

- FMLA compliance: Up to 12 weeks unpaid leave for serious health conditions
- Paid medical leave: 3 days for medical procedures
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for immediate family

### Jury Duty & Civic Engagement

- Paid leave for jury duty service
- One paid day off per year for voting
- Community service volunteer days: 2 days annually

### Personal Leave

- Emergency personal leave available with manager approval
- Unpaid leave available for personal reasons (up to 30 days/year)

## Work From Home Policy

### Eligibility

- Full-time employees in non-public-facing roles
- Performance review score of "Meets Expectations" or higher
- Manager and HR approval required

### Hybrid Work Arrangement

- Standard: 3 days office, 2 days remote
- Can be negotiated based on role and team needs
- Core hours: 10 AM - 3 PM Pacific Time

### Work From Home Guidelines

- Dedicated, quiet workspace with security
- High-speed internet (minimum 25 Mbps download)
- Secure VPN connection required
- Regular video calls for meetings
- Monthly in-person team collaboration days

## Remote Work

### Remote Work Eligibility

- Same requirements as work from home
- Requires executive approval
- Limited to 2 roles maximum per department

### Remote Work Requirements

- Must maintain regular communication with team
- Attend quarterly in-person meetings (company paid travel)
- Same VPN and security requirements as work from home
- 24-hour notice for any extended travel

## Code of Conduct

### Professional Behavior

- Treat all colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintain professional communication in all interactions
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflicts
- Maintain confidentiality of company and customer information

### Harassment & Discrimination

- Zero-tolerance policy for harassment or discrimination
- Immediate investigation of all complaints
- Protection against retaliation for good-faith complaints
- Anonymous reporting available through Ethics Hotline

### Social Media Policy

- Do not post confidential company information
- Do not make disparaging remarks about company or colleagues
- Disclose when posting about company with disclaimer
- Personal accounts are private but can affect professional reputation

## Performance Management

### Performance Reviews

- Conducted annually in December
- 360-degree feedback included
- Used for promotion, salary adjustment, and development decisions
- Appeal process available

### Performance Expectations

- Meet agreed-upon goals and deadlines
- Maintain professional development
- Demonstrate company values
- Contribute to team success

### Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

- Offered when performance does not meet expectations
- 30/60/90-day progression
- Clear goals and support resources provided
- Regular check-ins with HR and manager

## Termination

### Voluntary Resignation

- Two weeks' notice required
- Final paycheck includes unused PTO
- Exit interview conducted by HR
- References provided for positive employment history

### Involuntary Termination

- Severance package based on tenure and position
- COBRA continuation coverage available
- Outplacement services for management positions
- Final paycheck and PTO payment issued promptly

## Company Values

- **Innovation**: Embrace change and creative problem-solving
- **Excellence**: Deliver high-quality work with attention to detail
- **Integrity**: Act with honesty and transparency
- **Collaboration**: Work together as one team
- **Care**: Support the well-being of our team and community
